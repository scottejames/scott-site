Horizon

My ultimate todo app.

Horizon sorts everything I mean to do by when I’ll do it: Today, Tomorrow, Next Week or Someday. That keeps the Today list short enough to actually finish.

Adding a task takes one line. Typing Call tile supplier !high #KIT tomorrow @work sets the priority, project, day and whether it’s personal or work in one go.

It keeps personal and work lists apart, lets you add progress notes to a task, and installs on your phone like an app.

Beth's Gang

A set of tools to help neurospicy folks get along.

Small helpers that each do one job well. Break an overwhelming task into steps you can actually start, check how a message will land before you send it, sort a brain dump into something usable, or pick something from your dopamine menu.

There’s a study section too, with an assignment breakdown, an essay planner and a phrase bank. Many of the tools use Claude behind the scenes.
