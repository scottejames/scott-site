Scott James

I run developer experience engineering at an asset management firm in London. I don't get paid to write code any more, so my job now is making life better for the people who do.

Outside work I walk long distances, run parkrun and help out with Scouts in Mid Sussex.

About me

Work

I'm a programmer who became a leader but never stopped caring about the craft. I lead the team whose job is to make developers' lives better: faster feedback, fewer obstacles, tools that get out of the way.

I believe in mechanical sympathy. You build better software when you understand what the machine underneath is actually doing.

On foot

I did plenty of ultra hikes years ago, then let things slide. Two years ago I set out to exercise every single day. I've lost a lot of weight, I'm now a regular parkrunner, and I recently finished a 40 mile hike.

Scouting

I was a Scout leader with 10th Haywards Heath for ten years, then spent about a year with 1st Haywards Heath Scouts. These days I don't hold a regular section. I help wherever a group needs me: recently a few months with Beavers, and soon some time with Cubs.

I run the hike training programme for Mid Sussex and get involved with JOTI.

Code and AI

Every December I do Advent of Code, and I'm sad it looks like it's coming to an end.

I'm very excited about what AI can do, and a little terrified of where it takes us. I hope programmers don't end up as human-in-the-loop merge approvers.
