About

I’m a computer programmer who no longer gets paid to program computers. I’m a leader now, but I’m still very engaged with the work.

Work

I work in London, often from home in Haywards Heath, for a large asset management firm. I run developer experience engineering. My job is to make developers’ lives better.

I care a lot about mechanical sympathy: understanding the machine you’re building on makes you a better engineer, whatever layer you work at.

Code and AI

I do Advent of Code every year, and I’m sad that it appears to be coming to an end.

I’m very excited about AI and what it can do, and also somewhat terrified of where it takes us. Will programmers become human-in-the-loop merge approvers? Let’s hope not.

On foot

For the past two years I’ve been on a fitness journey, with the goal of exercising every day. I took part in quite a few ultra hikes before I let myself go. Since then I’ve lost a lot of weight, become a parkrunner, and recently completed a 40 mile hike.

Scouting

Most of my Scouting has been with the Scouts section. I was a Scout leader with 10th Haywards Heath for ten years, and more recently spent about a year with 1st Haywards Heath Scouts.

I’m now in a sort of retirement: no regular commitment, but I help out wherever groups need my skills. Very recently that meant a few months with Beavers, and I’m about to spend some time with Cubs.

I also look after the hike training programme for Mid Sussex and do other things like JOTI (Jamboree on the Internet).

Home

I live in Haywards Heath with my wife Anna. We have three children: Tom, Beth and Maddie.
